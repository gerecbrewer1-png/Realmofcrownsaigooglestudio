# Game Design Document

## 1. Vision & Core Loop

Players assume the mantle of Lord of an ancestral holding in the fractured realm of Valerius.
The fundamental progression loop:

```
[HARVEST / PRODUCE] ──> [UPGRADE BUILDINGS] ──> [EXPAND CAPABILITY]
         ▲                                                │
         │                                                ▼
   [CLAIM QUESTS]   <──  [RESEARCH & TRAIN]  <──  [UNLOCK MILESTONES]
```

## 2. Resources

1. **Food**: Feeds civilian populace and military units; required for training and tier upgrades.
2. **Wood**: Primary structural material for economic plots, archery ranges, and perimeter palisades.
3. **Stone**: Fortification masonry for high-tier Castle walls, warehouses, and quarries.
4. **Iron**: Weapon and armor smithing for heavy infantry, knights, and siege engines.
5. **Gold**: High-tier research, academy advancements, and hero skills.
6. **Gems**: Configurable premium currency obtained via gameplay (achievements, daily login, quest milestones) or optional store packs. Used for instant time acceleration, shield extensions, and convenience.

## 3. Data-Driven Building Archetypes

- **Main Castle (Keep)**: Realm center. Governs maximum building levels and unlock tiers.
- **Economic Production**:
  - Farm (Food generation)
  - Lumber Mill (Wood generation)
  - Quarry (Stone generation)
  - Iron Mine (Iron generation)
  - Gold Mine (Gold generation)
- **Logistics & Protection**:
  - Warehouse (Protects a fixed quota of resources against enemy plunder)
  - City Wall (Shields kingdom from direct incursions, houses defensive towers)
  - Hospital (Tends to wounded combatants)
- **Military & Science**:
  - Barracks (Infantry training)
  - Archery Range (Ranged unit training)
  - Stable (Cavalry breeding and cavalry training)
  - Siege Workshop (Catapults, trebuchets, and rams)
  - Academy (Technological innovations)

## 4. Fair Play & Retention Mechanics

- No paywall blocking progression: all buildings, military tiers, and technologies can be acquired through strategic gameplay and patience.
- Speed-ups are awarded regularly via quest lines and daily milestones.
- Novice Peace Shield (24 Hours) safeguards newly minted kingdoms during their initial expansion.
