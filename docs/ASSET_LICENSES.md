# Realm of Crowns — Complete Asset License Manifest

This document records the licensing, provenance, creator, commercial use permissions, download source locations, and active engine paths for all third-party and imported assets integrated into the Godot 4.7 Realm of Crowns runtime.

In strict accordance with project rules, all imported assets are verified CC0 or MIT licenses permitting commercial and non-commercial development, modification, and distribution. No ripped, proprietary, or non-commercial assets are permitted.
