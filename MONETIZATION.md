# Monetization & Store Specification

## Philosophy
Monetization in Realm of Crowns is designed around convenience, time acceleration, customization, and aesthetic expression—**never** inescapable pay-to-win choke points.

## 1. Gem Store Catalog
- Tier 1: Pouch of Gems (500 Gems) - $0.99
- Tier 2: Chest of Gems (2,750 Gems) - $4.99
- Tier 3: Sovereign Vault (6,000 Gems) - $9.99
- Tier 4: Royal Treasury (13,000 Gems) - $19.99
- Tier 5: King's Hoard (35,000 Gems) - $49.99
- Tier 6: Emperor's Cache (75,000 Gems) - $99.99

*All values are stored in server-side configuration tables and adjustable via the Admin Panel.*

## 2. Fair Earning Paths
Free-to-play players can obtain generous quantities of Gems through:
- First-time settlement charter (1,000 Gems)
- Chapter Milestone Quests (100–500 Gems per milestone)
- Daily Quest completions (50–150 Gems daily)
- Achievement milestones (Up to 10,000 Gems total)
- World Map NPC camp clears and seasonal events

## 3. Cosmetics & Convenience
- Custom Castle visual themes (e.g., Obsidian Citadel, Sunfire Spire).
- Avatar frames and heraldic crest banners.
- Extra builder queues and gathering march speedups.
